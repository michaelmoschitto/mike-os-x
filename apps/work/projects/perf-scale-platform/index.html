Perf Scale Platform

What is it?

Perf Scale Platform is a control room for large-scale simulations. Teams can run realistic customer workflows, watch the system respond, and find bottlenecks in one place.

My role

I built across the full performance-testing lifecycle: realistic customer scenarios, the tools that ran them, and the views teams used to understand what happened. My work includes the dashboard, Automation Runs experience, real-time NPC tracking, and database monitoring. I also partnered across Product, Platform, and Infrastructure to make scale testing self-service.
